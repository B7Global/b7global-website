About the company

A clear point of contact for your commercial requests

B7 Global Brokerage Services is a single-owner limited liability company operating from the United Arab Emirates. We support business communication and help coordinate introductions between companies, product and service providers, and prospective customers, within the scope of our licence and any applicable approvals.

Our vision

To be a trusted partner for building practical, lasting business relationships between companies in the UAE and regional and international markets.

Our mission

To make relevant commercial opportunities easier to access through clear communication, a sound understanding of business needs, and coordinated follow-up from initial introduction to agreed next steps.

Our values

  • Clarity and integrity in commercial communication.
  • Responsiveness and reliable follow-up.
  • Understanding client needs before proposing a route forward.
  • Respect for business confidentiality.
  • Professional relationships built for the long term.

How we work

A clear process from first call to next steps

We follow simple, defined steps that organise communication and align expectations between the parties.

  1. 1

    Understand the requirement

    Confirm the product or service, the target market, and the client's key requirements.

  2. 2

    Identify a suitable party

    Look for a supplier, customer or business partner that fits the agreed scope.

  3. 3

    Arrange the introduction

    Coordinate an introduction and initial information exchange, with the parties' agreement.

  4. 4

    Follow up on commercial points

    Track inquiries, documents and the commercial steps the parties agree to exchange.

  5. 5

    Confirm next steps

    Record agreed actions and follow up with the relevant parties.
